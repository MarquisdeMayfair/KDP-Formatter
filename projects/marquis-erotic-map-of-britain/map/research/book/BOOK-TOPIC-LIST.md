## Notes on coverage

- **All 107 map locations are covered exactly once.** 97 public + 10 London Taboo.
- **Book chapters deliberately differ from site categories.** Mên-an-Tol, St Winefride's Well and Padstow 'Obby 'Oss sit in *Notable Landmarks* on the site but belong with the folk rites in the book; Highland Mary, Stonehenge and Tintagel move from *Tragic Romance* into the literary chapter. The web versions still file under their existing categories — only the book reorders.
- **13 topics are ADAPT jobs** (10 taboo + Buttermere + Ffynnon Elian + Dancing Marquess), so they run faster and cheaper than the word counts suggest.
- **`Al Fresco & Wild` has no locations at all** — a live but empty category on the site. If you want it filled, it needs new research commissioned before it can have topics.
