## Back cover

### THE EROTIC MAP OF BRITAIN

#### A Tour of the Places Where Britain Misbehaved

Britain has a reputation for keeping its bedroom door shut. The evidence says otherwise — and the evidence, it turns out, has an address.

From a Dorset hillside with an embarrassing secret about its own age to a Yorkshire diary of an estimated five million words, written in a code that remained private for decades. From licensed brothels from which a bishop drew rent to a village eight miles over the Scottish border that turned a loophole in English law into a national industry. From molly houses and pleasure gardens to a Chelsea basement behind a green door, and a Soho rule holding that a naked woman was perfectly decent so long as she did not move.

The Marquis de Mayfair maps a thousand years of British desire — the scandals and elopements, the courtships and raids, the censors who always arrived too late and the lovers who were always one street ahead. Along the way he explodes some of the nation's most cherished myths about its own past and finds a truth far stranger underneath.

Meticulously researched. Entirely unembarrassed. Best read with a map, a drink and nobody looking over your shoulder.

## Website introduction

### The Erotic Map of Britain

An interactive guide to over a hundred real locations across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland where Britain did its loving, scheming, eloping and misbehaving — from Cornwall to Balmoral, and from Belfast to Bankside.

Explore Tudor palaces and Georgian pleasure gardens, chalk hill figures and holy wells, molly houses, literary houses, coaching inns and clubland basements. Every location carries checked dates, people, sources and coordinates, plus the story behind it — including which famous legends turn out to be Victorian invention.

Search by place, county, person or theme. Follow a curated route. Or simply find out what happened on your own doorstep.
