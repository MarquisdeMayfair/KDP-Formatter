# Artwork Generation Prompts — The Erotic Map of Britain

This is the single source of truth for all generated artwork. The programme contains 43 interior plates: ten chapter frontispieces plus one illustration for every other story (Topics 1, 3, 5 … 65). Create the cover components only after the interior has been typeset. Do not generate a finished wraparound cover until the final KDP page count is known.

## Approved visual system

**Decision:** Option C is the book's visual backbone. The more erotically charged entries use a controlled combination of Option B's cinematic realism and Option C's oxblood woodcut surface. Empty architecture, symbolic still lifes, faux-Victorian borders and generic parchment illustration are rejected.

### Direction C — oxblood woodcut

> Handmade wood engraving crossed with expressive contemporary linocut and drypoint. Bold charcoal-black cut marks, rough fields, scratched highlights, imperfect registration and tactile ink on warm fibrous paper. Use oxblood as the main second ink, with sparing antique-gold and verdigris hand-tinted accents. The result must look authored by a printmaker, not like polished digital fantasy art. House palette: midnight navy #0B1424, oxblood #6E1F2A, antique gold #C5A15A, warm parchment #E8DDC7, ivory #F5F0E6, charcoal #252420, with restrained verdigris #557A73 accents.

### Direction B+C — cinematic woodcut hybrid

> Cinematic historical realism translated through the same handmade woodcut system: believable adult faces, natural hands, lived-in period fabrics, human proximity and prestige-drama lighting, finished with expressive charcoal drypoint, oxblood linocut overprint, scratched highlights and tactile paper grain. Preserve photographic depth and emotional immediacy without glossy advertising polish or AI-perfect symmetry. House palette: midnight navy #0B1424, oxblood #6E1F2A, antique gold #C5A15A, warm parchment #E8DDC7, ivory #F5F0E6, charcoal #252420, with restrained verdigris #557A73 accents.

**Palette:**

- Midnight navy — `#0B1424`
- Oxblood — `#6E1F2A`
- Antique gold — `#C5A15A`
- Warm parchment — `#E8DDC7`
- Ivory — `#F5F0E6`
- Charcoal — `#252420`
- Verdigris accent — `#557A73`
- Selective passion-red punctuation — `#D7192D` (assigned C plates only; never more than 3–5% of the frame)

**Story rule:** Every plate must show people, ritual, desire, jeopardy or a visible human consequence. Architecture anchors the place but does not become the subject. Charged plates use adult faces, hands, closeness, curtains, clothing, private rooms and aftermath to create commercial heat while remaining consensual, non-explicit and suitable for KDP.

**Selective-red rule:** On specifically assigned C plates, one story object may break the house palette in saturated passion red #D7192D. Treat it like a selective-colour noir punctuation mark: small, intentional and dramatically brighter than the surrounding oxblood. Never add red to an unassigned plate; never use it as blood, gore, a general colour grade or empty decoration.

**Consistency rules:** The same oxblood, charcoal and fibrous-paper print surface runs through both directions. Use period-accurate architecture, landscape and clothing. No modern objects unless the prompt explicitly calls for a present-day scene. Adults only; never eroticise coercion, distress or victims. No text, logos, watermarks, pseudo-writing, malformed hands or decorative AI clutter.

**Interior production spec:** 3600 × 2700 px · 4:3 landscape · RGB · 300-DPI print placement · no bleed · central safe composition. Save exactly as `plates/plate-NNN.png`. Preserve a quiet outer margin and keep faces, hands and important architecture away from trim edges and the gutter.

**Quality check for every image:** correct period and location; palette compliance; no accidental text; no malformed faces or hands; no visible contemporary objects; no important detail in the crop zone.
